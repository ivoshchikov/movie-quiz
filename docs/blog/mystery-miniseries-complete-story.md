# 10 Mystery Miniseries with a Complete Story

Roadmap item N06. Canonical path `/blog/mystery-miniseries-complete-story`. Editorial and U.S. catalog checks on November 27, 2026 (Asia/Nicosia).

The reader chooses among an answer to a whodunit, a revelation about someone close to the investigator, and the outcome of a dramatized real case. The selection has no title in common with the general completed-miniseries guide. Evidence of closure was reviewed separately from the copy so that the guide can state the kind of ending without revealing how it arrives. We used `Dear Child` and `The Breakthrough` in place of `Bodies` and `The Night Of`. The former two give firmer closure under the guide's criterion. The `Bodies` creator describes leaving an opening for a continuation, and `The Night Of` ends the legal case while leaving the identity of the killer unresolved.
