The [Cinema United calendar](https://cinemaunited.org/release-calendar/) lists the first nine films. GKIDS dates the select-theater opening of Koreeda's live-action *Look Back* to December 30 and its nationwide expansion to January 15, 2027. Warner Bros. lists Dune Insider screenings on December 14 and 15. IMAX's 70mm venue list is a separate source for screening format. The January 2027 date previously announced for *The Angry Birds Movie 3* was superseded by [Rovio's December 23 announcement](https://www.rovio.com/articles/mrbeast-and-salish-matter-join-the-flock-in-the-angry-birds-movie-3-in-theaters-december-23-2026/). Sony's U.S. schedule, Cinemark and Cinema United agree on December 25 for *Jumanji: Open World*.
