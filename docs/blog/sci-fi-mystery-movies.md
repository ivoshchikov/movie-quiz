# N05 — 10 Sci-Fi Mystery Movies

Prepared for the authorized November 6, 2026 publication. Canonical: https://hard-quiz.com/blog/sci-fi-mystery-movies. Release and post-deployment verification are recorded in the pull request and the owner's content plan after they complete.

The article helps a reader choose a standalone film by the kind of mystery, pace and runtime. The ten selections are Arrival, Moon, Coherence, Source Code, Ex Machina, Dark City, Minority Report, Gattaca, The Thirteenth Floor and The Vast of Night. Notes stop at the premise and avoid explanations of endings. English public copy; Russian owner-facing plan and image brief.

## Editorial checks and decisions

- Ex Machina has an accessible Hulu landing page, but the current US offer list has no Hulu subscription and departure trackers report a November 1 removal. The article therefore chooses a confirmed digital rental/purchase route, cross-checked against the US Apple listing. No claim about current Hulu access is published.
- The source list in the entry links to official distributor materials, US catalog pages, SFFILM, a director interview and published criticism. JustWatch US cross-checks digital offers. Public pages were checked November 6; playback and purchases were not tested.
- Arrival links to Paramount+. Other titles link to separately paid digital routes, with Dark City specifically a purchase of the Director's Cut. Apple TV Store transactions are distinguished from the subscription. No prices or permanent availability claims.
- Dark City recommends the 111-minute Director's Cut, whose opening preserves the mystery. Arrow US confirms 111/100-minute editions; Proyas discusses the added theatrical narration in Paul Rowlands's interview. The theatrical catalog must not be used as evidence for the longer edition.
- Coherence uses film year 2013, supported by SFFILM; the US store says 2014. Festival runtime is 89 minutes, Apple rounds to 88. The article explains both.
- The Vast of Night uses US release year 2020, with its 2019 festival premiere explained. About 90 minutes accommodates 89–91-minute listings. November 5–6 US offers show rentals and purchases; its original Amazon distribution is not taken as proof of a current Prime subscription.
- Minority Report uses the Apple US 145-minute listing, corroborated by BFI. Some distributor materials round to 146.
- Original artwork is drawn by `artwork/sci-fi-mystery.py`. It is decorative editorial artwork, not a film frame. Five WebP assets provide the cover, three responsive listing sizes and a separate social preview. Ten film stills will be added after the owner supplies them; the published article has no empty image slots.
- Internal links lead to the published 2026 sci-fi list, Knives Out recommendations and the widescreen explainer. The existing footer selects a game; it makes no claim that these films occur in the question bank.
