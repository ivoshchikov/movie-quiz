# Real film media correction — 9 November 2026

The owner expected actual film posters or stills with documented conditions of use.
The earlier publication of 73 inline geometric illustrations did not meet that request.
The owner explicitly chose to omit an image temporarily wherever permission cannot
be confirmed, preserving the article text and links.

## Result prepared for publication

- 46 real posters from the US Apple iTunes Search/Lookup catalog, matched by film,
  director and edition. Each poster has its own exact returned store link and the
  approved unmodified “Get it on iTunes” badge in the same figure.
- Two real frames from *Tears of Steel* (2012), by Blender Foundation / Project Mango,
  licensed CC BY 3.0. Visible captions link to the source and license, identify the
  creator, and describe resizing/compression or deliberate centered comparison crops.
- 25 unconfirmed image slots omitted. Their film/series text, facts and existing links remain.
- All 219 responsive files belonging to the 73 inline geometric illustrations retired.
  The three original guide title cards and other existing original covers remain.
- Conditional posters and credited film frames never enter generic cover, social or
  fallback gallery metadata. Failed list covers retain a usable text card.

## Conditions and limits

Apple catalog artwork is conditional promotional material, not a general stock-image
license and not a blanket film-still license. These figures promote the corresponding
US store items; the site does not add affiliate tags. Artwork stays with the matching
purchase page and badge, must not be reused in unrelated promotion, and must be removed
if authorization or availability changes. Only whole-image proportional resizing and
WebP compression are applied. Dark City is explicitly identified as the Director’s Cut;
its store edition date is not confused with the original 1998 film year. Older and newer
remakes are never substituted on a title-only match.

The CC BY 3.0 frames may be used commercially under that license with attribution,
license link and change notices. Their native frame is 3840×1600 (2.40:1); the guide
honestly labels the 2.39:1 and 16:9 comparison crops. No endorsement is implied.

Netflix’s public press application is not photo-use approval. No press applications,
rights-holder messages, purchases or paid licenses are made in this task. The omitted
images can be restored when a matching permission, appropriate licensed asset or
verified conditional promotional source becomes available.

Sources, returned store identities, lookup dates, source hashes, derivative hashes,
dimensions, processing notes and omission reasons are recorded in
`real-film-media-2026-11-09.json`. `image-rights-ledger.json` is the active asset inventory;
`original-artwork-manifest.json` now lists only active original guide covers. Historical
insertion records and the previous retirement report are not current permission claims.

This documents a concrete permission basis and its implementation. It does not promise
zero copyright complaints, Google rankings or approval by an advertising partner.

## Verification

The provenance gate checks local file identity, retired-file absence, article scope,
matching poster/badge destination, and visible CC attribution. The new negative tests
reject missing credits, wrong purchase links and reuse in generic cover/gallery slots.
Local checks: typecheck, lint and build passed; 50 publication tests and 106 targeted browser scenarios passed; the local
publication verifier checked 26 pages and 216 images with zero errors.
Production revision, CI and HTTP verification are recorded separately after deployment.
This change covers editorial blog images and their placement.
