# 10 Completed Miniseries to Watch Over a Weekend

Editorial record for the November 1, 2026 publication of `/blog/completed-miniseries-to-watch`. The planned November 4 slot was brought forward at the owner's request. English public copy; US viewing scope. This is a spoiler-free selection by mood and available time, not a scored ranking or a claim of firsthand screenings.

## Selection and closure

The ten working candidates were retained: Unorthodox; When They See Us; Chernobyl; Normal People; Five Days at Memorial; The Queen's Gambit; One Day; Godless; Station Eleven; Maid. Official Netflix pages retain limited-series labels for their six selections; HBO/Warner and Apple identify their limited-series formats, and Hulu confirms twelve episodes of Normal People. Released catalogs and completed episode guides were checked. Normal People's intentionally open ending is disclosed; “complete” is not a promise that all characters' futures are settled or that a producer could never revisit the material.

Genres include romance, historical and social drama, speculative fiction and a western. No candidate overlaps the ten-title working list for the later mystery-miniseries article. Violence, abuse, distressing hospital material and other tone limits are described without revealing endings.

## Running-time method

The [episode-minute record](completed-miniseries-episode-minutes.json) preserves every input, the source family, raw sum and rounded display total. Whole-minute listings are summed, then rounded to the nearest five minutes; the UI uses the same arrays for table and section facts. No average duration is multiplied by an episode count. Adverts, breaks, trailers, interviews and separate bonus material are excluded. Different platform rounding and edits can produce differences of a few minutes; the guide explicitly describes these as planning estimates.

Reader-facing official title pages and specific catalog links are retained in the article's sources.
